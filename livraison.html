Support

Shipping

Orders ship from Montreal, Quebec. Tracking is emailed as soon as your parcel leaves the warehouse.

Delivery times

  • Quebec: 3 to 5 business days
  • Rest of Canada: 5 to 10 business days
  • United States & international: quoted at checkout (typically 7 to 15 business days)

Preorders ship when the piece is ready — not with in-stock items.

Rates

Shipping is calculated from your address at checkout. Free shipping on orders of $150 CAD and over (threshold can change).

Tracking

You get a tracking number by email when the order ships. If nothing shows after a few days, write us with your order number.

Contact

Questions about a delivery? We reply within 24h.
